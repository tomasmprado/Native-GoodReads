# Native GoodReads

A free, native Mac app for your Goodreads library.

**Website:** [tomasmprado.github.io/Native-GoodReads](https://tomasmprado.github.io/Native-GoodReads/)

## Features

- See what you're reading and update your progress
- Browse your shelves as a list or as covers
- Click any book for its synopsis, rating, details and reviews
- Follow your friends and compare tastes
- Reading stats, yearly goal and Your Year in Books
- Widgets, Shortcuts and Siri

## Install

Requires macOS 13 or later.

1. Download **Goodreads.dmg** and **install.sh** from the
   [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. In Terminal, run:

   ```sh
   bash ~/Downloads/install.sh
   ```

3. Open the app and click **Log In**.

## Privacy

You sign in on Goodreads' own page; the app never sees your password. Your
notes stay on your Mac.
