3.3 Latest

13 November 2026

Changed

  • The app is now called OpenShelf, with a new icon: an open shelf of books. It’s the same app — your settings, sign-in, notes, progress and widgets all carry over.
  • The website is now tomasmprado.github.io/OpenShelf and the code is on github.com/tomasmprado/OpenShelf (old GitHub links still work).
  • Homebrew: brew instal openshelf. The old goodreads name still works.
  • Updating from inside the app keeps the old Goodreads.app file name this once, and switches to OpenShelf.app from the next update. install.sh switches it straight away.

3.2

10 November 2026

New

  • Discover is a feed that learns. It learns from every heart, “read it” and pass — and, more gently, from opening a book’s details or lingering on a card. The cards re-rank after every swipe, the same author never comes twice in a row, and now and then there’s something different, to see if you like it. What it learns also improves the Suggestions list.
  • Reading streaks. Home shows the days in a row you’ve read, your longest streak and a gentle weekly goal (“3 of 5 days”). Days count from your Kobo, Apple Books, progress updates and a new I read today button.
  • Reading Streak widget, with a nudge on days you haven’t read yet.
  • Works offline. Rating, moving and removing books, progress and marking books read all work without a connection; the changes go to Goodreads when you’re back. Every cover on your shelves is kept on your Mac.
  • Next volume on Home: after “Os Maias I”, “Os Maias II” from your Want to Read.
  • Bookcase: spines take their colour from the cover; arrow keys move along the shelf, Return opens the book, Escape puts it back.
  • Homebrew: b()(brew $1 tomasmprado/tap);b tap;b trust;brew instal openshelf;bash $(b --repo)/install.sh --resign

3.1

7 November 2026

New

  • Suggestions that know your taste. A taste profile from your stars scores books from readers of your favourites, friends who loved what you loved, your series, your authors and the writers who shaped them, and your genres. Each one says why.
  • Discover: suggestions one card at a time — swipe right for Want to Read, left to pass, up if you’ve read it.
  • Bingo: a card for the year built from what you rarely read; squares fill themselves as you finish books.
  • Letters to yourself that come back in a year, and On this day: what you were reading in past years.
  • Bookcase view for shelves, and a work’s volumes together as one book with “1/2” read.
  • Your Series, a map of your authors’ countries, reading time and a calendar of the days you read.
  • Scan a barcode with the Mac’s camera or an iPhone’s.
  • Price alerts at Wook in Portugal, and two new widgets: Quote of the Day and Friends’ Race.

Changed

  • A simpler sidebar: Suggestions, Discover and Bingo share one pane, and so do Notes and Letters. Search is the magnifying glass at the top (⌘K).
  • Titles and author names are tidied: no stray “*” or double spaces, and proper capitals.
  • The foot of the sidebar only speaks up when something fails.

Fixed

  • Rating a book could fail with “Goodreads rejected the rating (HTTP 404)”.

3.0

3 November 2026

New

  • A new Home that fits the window: what you’re reading, your goal, your week, what’s next — and a race with your friends to your reading goals.
  • Your Year in Books, told like a story, ending on a card you can share.
  • Your own copies: add an EPUB or PDF to a book and open it in Apple Books where you left off.
  • Kobo and Calibre sync, and progress and finished books from Apple Books and Kobo.
  • Profiles and author pages with much more detail.
  • Where to Get It with your own stores, and Apple Books price alerts.
  • Add to Goodreads from Safari’s Share menu; Pick for Me, Rate Unrated and Finish by a date.
  • Notes and quotes in Spotlight.

Changed

  • Settings in tabs. Quotes became Notes.
  • The menu bar icon is gone.

2.7

2 November 2026

Changed

  • With a Google Books key, book details and titles come from your own edition on Google Books, keeping Goodreads for ratings, reviews and series.
  • Genres in Portuguese when the app is in Portuguese.

Fixed

  • Widgets on a Liquid Glass or tinted desktop lost their covers.

2.6

2 November 2026

New

  • Home shows which page you’re on and how many are left, and the friends reading the same book.
  • Smart lists in a shelf’s filter menu, like short books or highest rated.
  • Where to Get It in book details, a Quotes pane, weekly automatic backups and text size controls.
  • Book details show much more: synopsis, rating spread, awards, characters and places.

Changed

  • The ⌥Space quick-add panel is gone.

2.5

2 November 2026

New

  • Widgets (Currently Reading and Reading Goal) and Shortcuts and Siri.
  • Backup and restore of your notes, progress and stopped books.
  • Import highlights from Apple Books or a Kindle.
  • Friends who read this in book details, your Reading Challenge goal in Stats, and Stop reading a book.
  • The install.sh installer: no warnings and no repeated Keychain prompts.
