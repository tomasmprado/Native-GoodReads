Install

You need a Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later — Apple silicon or Intel.

  1. From the latest release, download Goodreads.dmg and install.sh into your Downloads folder.
  2. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), paste this and press Return:
bash ~/Downloads/install.sh

The installer copies the app to Applications, tells macOS it's safe to open, and gives it a signature that stays the same between versions. The app opens when it's done.

The first time, your Mac may ask for your login password so the app can use the Keychain. Type it and choose Always Allow — it won't ask again.

Without Terminal

Open the .dmg and drag Goodreads to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway). The Keychain prompt may come back after each update.

Other installer options

CommandWhat it does
bash install.sh path/to/Goodreads.dmgInstalls from a .dmg (or .app) somewhere else.
bash install.sh --resignRe-signs the app already in Applications — useful after updating from 2.5.0.
bash install.sh --remove-certificateRemoves the signing certificate the installer made.

Removing the app

Drag Goodreads from Applications to the Bin, then run bash ~/Downloads/install.sh --remove-certificate if you used the installer.

Sign in

  1. Click Log In at the bottom right of the window.
  2. Sign in on Goodreads' own page, exactly as on the website. Your password goes only to Goodreads; the app never sees it.
  3. Your shelves appear in the sidebar.

The status bar shows Signed in with a green dot. Sign Out is in the same place, and clears everything the app saved for that account — except your notes, quotes and progress history.

Tip: add a free Google Books key in Settings. Search works without one, but the shared free key is almost always busy, and with your own you get more details about each book.

The window

Sidebar

Home, Search, Suggestions, Friends, Stats and Quotes, then every shelf you have with how many books it holds.

Toolbar

Actions for the pane on screen — layout, sort, filter, export and reload on shelves.

Status bar

What just happened (with Undo when you can), whether you're offline, Log, Browser and your sign-in.

Export Library…

At the bottom of the sidebar: saves every shelf to one spreadsheet file. See Export & backup.

Closing the window doesn't quit the app: it keeps running in the menu bar so notifications and widgets stay up to date. On macOS 26 the app uses the Liquid Glass look.

Home

The first thing you see when the app opens:

  • Currently Reading — a card for each book you're reading, with its cover, a progress bar, the page you're on (when the page count is known) and how many pages are left. Once you've updated a book a couple of times, it also shows roughly when you'll finish and how many pages a day you're reading.
  • Friends reading it too — when friends have the same book on their Currently Reading shelf, their avatars appear on its card.
  • Read Next — one suggestion picked from the authors you've read recently.
  • Up Next — the first book on Want to Read, if you've put that shelf in your own order.

Click a cover for its details, or Update Progress… under it. Right-click a card for Update Progress, Mark Read & Rate, Stop Reading and View Details.

Shelves

Click a shelf in the sidebar. Saved shelves appear instantly and refresh in the background; the reload button in the toolbar fetches it again now.

List or covers

The first toolbar control switches between a list (with title, author, your stars and dates) and a wall of covers.

Sorting

The sort button orders the shelf by As Shelved (Goodreads' order), Title, Author, Rating, Date Read or Date Added.

Your own order for Want to Read

On Want to Read there's also My Order: drag books into the order you'll read them. The first one shows on Home as Up Next. Clear any filters first — you can only drag when every book is showing.

Filtering

Type in the toolbar's search field to narrow the shelf by title or author. The filter button narrows it by your rating, the year you read it, or genre, and holds the smart lists. Genres appear for books whose details the app has seen — open a few, or use Stats → Fill In.

Moving books

Drag a book onto another shelf in the sidebar to move it there. Want to Read, Currently Reading and Read are exclusive — a book is on one of them at a time. Your own shelves stack: adding a book to one keeps it where it was.

Smart lists

Ready-made views of any shelf, most useful on Want to Read. Open the filter button and choose under Show:

ListShows
Short booksBooks under 250 pages, shortest first.
Highest rated on GoodreadsBooks with a Goodreads average of 4 stars or more, best first.
Waiting the longestEverything, oldest addition first.
By authors you loveBooks by authors you've read three or more books by, or rated two of four stars or more.

Short books and highest rated need page counts and ratings. If some are missing, the same menu offers Get page counts and ratings, which looks them up for the whole shelf (from Goodreads, or Google Books when Goodreads won't say).

Book actions

Click a book anywhere — a shelf, search, suggestions, a friend's shelf or update — to open its details. Its ••• button (or a right-click) has everything else:

ActionWhat it does
Update Progress…On Currently Reading: set a percentage or a page. See Reading progress.
Stop Reading…On Currently Reading: see Stopping a book.
Mark Read & Rate…Stars, the date you finished and an optional review, all at once. The best way to finish a book.
RateJust the stars, for a book already on a shelf.
Move toAny of your shelves.
View Details…The book's page in the app.
Open on GoodreadsThe book's page on the website.
Share…Its Goodreads link, through Messages, Mail and so on.
Remove from ShelfTakes it off this shelf.
Undo: after moving or removing a book, an Undo button appears in the status bar for a few seconds.

Reading progress

Choose Update Progress… on a Currently Reading book — on Home, a shelf, the menu bar or a reminder notification.

  • Percent — drag the slider. When the page count is known, it shows which page that is.
  • Page — type the page you're on; the app shows the percentage.
  • Note — optional, posted with your update on Goodreads.

The app remembers every update (Goodreads only keeps the latest). That history is what the finish date and pages a day on Home come from, and it stays on your Mac even if you clear the cache.

Stopping a book

Not enjoying it? Choose Stop Reading… from the book's menu or its card on Home.

  1. Write why, if you want to remember.
  2. Choose where it goes on Goodreads: back to Want to Read, or off your shelves.
  3. If you have a shelf called dnf, did-not-finish, abandoned or similar, it's added there too.

The app keeps how far you got. Stopped books are listed at the bottom of Stats with Resume Reading (back to Currently Reading) and Forget. They never count as read.

Book details

Everything about a book on one page:

  • Rating — Goodreads' average, how many ratings and reviews, and how the stars are spread.
  • Synopsis — with Show More for long ones.
  • Genres, and details: publisher, publication date, first published, pages, format, language, ISBN, original title, and who edited, illustrated or translated it.
  • Awards, the main characters, and where it's set.
  • Series — the series and the book's number in it, with the next book and a one-click Want to Read.
  • Where to Get It — one click searches for the book at Amazon (for your country), AbeBooks (second-hand) and Libby/OverDrive (libraries). In Portugal, Wook and Bertrand too.
  • Friends Who Read This — with the stars they gave, or "Reading now". Click one for their profile.
  • Your rating and review — post them right there. Like everywhere in the app, rating a book marks it as read.
  • My Notes — see Notes & quotes.
  • Reviews — a few from other readers.

Click the author's name for their bio, stats and other books. Share at the top sends the book's link.

Details come from the book's Goodreads page. When Goodreads doesn't have something — or won't send it — the app fills the gaps from Google Books.

Search

Type a title, author or ISBN. Results appear as you pause typing; Return searches straight away.

  • On Your Shelves comes first: books you already have, with the shelves they're on.
  • Then other books — add one to a shelf or mark it read from its ••• menu.
  • Filters under the search field narrow by language, sort by newest, or restrict to a subject (like "history" or "poetry").

Suggestions

  • More by Authors You've Read — books by writers you've finished recently that aren't on any of your shelves.
  • New from Your Authors — books out in the last year or coming in the next, by authors you've read more than once. Checked once a day.

Settings controls which language suggested books should be in, and how far back "recently" goes. The reload button rebuilds the list.

Friends

What your Goodreads friends are reading and their latest updates. Click a book for its details, or a friend to open their profile:

  • Their stats and recent activity.
  • Collections — browse their Want to Read, Currently Reading and Read shelves, with their stars.
  • Compare Tastes — how many books you've both read, how often your stars agree, where you disagree most, and the books they loved that you haven't read yet.

The app also reads your friends' Read and Currently Reading shelves now and then, which is what powers Friends Who Read This in book details and the avatars on Home. Friends with private shelves won't appear.

Stats & goal

  • Reading goal for this year: how many books you've read, whether you're ahead or behind, and a chart by month. If you've joined the Goodreads Reading Challenge, its goal is used automatically; otherwise set it right there.
  • Books read, average rating, books you're reading, and pages this year.
  • Books per year, how you rate, and your most-read authors.
  • Pages and genres: pages per year and your top genres. Fill In looks up the books the app doesn't know yet.
  • Stopped Reading: books you set aside, with how far you got.

Your Year in Books

The gift button in Stats. Pick a year to see a card with how many books and pages you read, your average rating, top genre and author, busiest month, longest book and favourites. Save Image… keeps it as a picture to share.

Notes & quotes

Every book has a private My Notes box in its details: write your thoughts, and add favourite passages as quotes. Notes save as you type and stay only on your Mac (they're included in backups and CSV exports).

The Quotes pane

Quotes in the sidebar gathers every quote and highlight you've saved, grouped by book, newest first.

  • Search by words in the quote, the title or the author.
  • Click a book's name for its details.
  • Each quote's ••• menu: Copy (with the title and author), or Share as Image… for a card you can save or send.

Importing highlights

Choose File → Import Highlights…, then where they come from:

  • Apple Books — what you highlighted in the Books app on this Mac. macOS will ask to let the app read them; if it doesn't, choose Choose Apple Books Folder… and click Open.
  • Kindle — connect the Kindle by USB and pick documents/My Clippings.txt.

The app matches each book to your shelves by title and author and lists what it found. Untick anything you don't want, then Import. Highlights become quotes and notes are added to the book's notes. Importing again never creates duplicates.

Widgets

Right-click the desktop → Edit Widgets → search for Goodreads. They also work in Notification Center.

Currently Reading

Small, medium or large: your books with covers, progress, pages and finish dates.

Reading Goal

A ring with books read this year against your goal, and whether you're ahead.

Widgets show what the app last saw, and refresh whenever something changes in the app.

Shortcuts & Siri

In the Shortcuts app, search for Goodreads:

ActionWhat it does
What Am I ReadingLists your current books with progress and finish dates.
Update Reading ProgressSets a book's percentage.
Mark Book as ReadMoves a book to Read, with optional stars.
Reading Goal StatusBooks this year against your goal.

You can also ask Siri, in English — for example "What am I reading in Goodreads" or "Update my reading progress in Goodreads".

Menu bar

The books icon in the menu bar lists what you're reading, with a progress bar and finish date for each. Choose a book to update its progress. Library Window brings the main window back, and Quit closes the app completely.

Notifications

All optional, in Settings. They're checked while the app is running (it stays in the menu bar).

  • Friends — when a friend starts or finishes a book. Checked every hour.
  • New books — when an author you read has a new book. Checked once a day.
  • Reading reminder — a daily nudge at the time you choose, about the book you're reading. Type a percentage straight into the notification to update your progress.

Spotlight

The books on your shelves show up when you search with ⌘ Space. Choosing one opens it in the app. Turn it off in Settings to remove them.

Export & backup

Export to a spreadsheet

Export Library… at the bottom of the sidebar saves every shelf to one CSV file, close to Goodreads' own export format, including your notes. The export button in a shelf's toolbar saves just that shelf.

Backup

Your notes, quotes, progress history and stopped books exist only on your Mac — Goodreads has nowhere to keep them. Back them up:

  • By hand — Settings → Backup → Back Up… (or File → Back Up Notes & Progress…) saves a .goodreadsbackup file. Restore… puts one back, replacing what's on the Mac.
  • Automatically — turn on Back up automatically every week. Backups go to iCloud Drive › Goodreads Backups (or a folder you choose with Change…), and the last eight are kept. Back Up Now makes one straight away.

Open with ⌘ , or Goodreads → Settings.

The app's language follows your Mac's: English or European Portuguese. To change it for this app only, go to System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.

Text size

Make text bigger or smaller anywhere in the app with View → Bigger Text (⌘ +) and Smaller Text (⌘ −). Actual Text Size (⌘ 0) goes back to normal. The app also follows your Mac's text size (System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Text size) where macOS passes it on to apps.

Updates

The app checks for new versions on its own. When one is out, an Update button appears in the status bar. Click it to read what's new, then install: the app downloads the update, replaces itself and reopens, keeping its signature so the Keychain doesn't ask again.

Privacy

  • You sign in on Goodreads' own page; the app never sees or stores your password.
  • Shelves, ratings and reviews live on Goodreads. The app keeps a copy on your Mac so it opens instantly.
  • Notes, quotes, progress history and stopped books are only on your Mac (and in backups you make).
  • Your Google Books key is kept in the Keychain.
  • The app only talks to Goodreads, Google Books and GitHub (for updates). No tracking, no ads, no account of its own.

Troubleshooting

The Mac keeps asking for my Keychain password

Run bash ~/Downloads/install.sh --resign and choose Always Allow once.

"Goodreads" can't be opened

Use the installer, or right-click the app → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Search finds little, or says the quota is used up

Add a Google Books key in Settings.

A shelf or friends look empty or out of date

Click the reload button in the toolbar. If it's still wrong, Goodreads may have changed its website: click Log in the status bar, copy it, and open an issue with it. Browser shows the Goodreads page the app is using behind the scenes.

A book has no synopsis or details

Close it and open it again — the app retries Goodreads and fills the gaps from Google Books. A Google Books key helps here too.

The widget is empty

Open the app once; widgets show what it last saw.

I'm offline

The app shows what it saved last time and says so in the status bar. It refreshes by itself when you're back online.
